---
title: Device
description: "Represents a MIDI or audio device on a track or in a rack chain."
---

Represents a MIDI or audio device on a track or in a rack chain. The `Device`
class is the base for every Live and third-party device — its automatable
parameters, on/off state, and preset-loading behavior are shared by all
device subclasses.
